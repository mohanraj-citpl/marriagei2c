How to arrive

Take MTC bus 6D.

Board Metropolitan Transport Corporation bus 6D. Step down at the Cemetery Road stop. The mandapam is No.1 Cemetery Road, Royapuram.

The 6D runs from Tollgate through Tondiarpet and Washermenpet, past R.S.R.M. Hospital and Royapuram, then on through Beach, Parrys, and Marina toward Foreshore Estate and Thiruvanmiyur.

R.S.R.M. Hospital and Royapuram are the stops beside Cemetery Road, if you need a landmark.
